# Standing facts

Facts the DM has ruled on during chapter review, with the session of the ruling. Everything here is known to the players; this file is in the public repo. Session-specific answers go to `build/.chronicle/rulings.jsonl` instead. Append, never silently rewrite.

## Places

- Tiryki Anchorage lies outside Port Nyanzaru's southeastern wall, where the River Tiryki fans into its delta and meets the Bay of Chult. (s32)
- The Seething Halls are the Obanashi temple in a mountain flank between the Soshenstar and the Tiryki. The company met the woman then called Nerissa in its entry hall. The chronicle calls the place "the temple at the Seething Halls". (s30, s32)
- The Soshenstar is slow and brown; the Tiryki comes down fast and steep out of a mist-shrouded canyon with waterfalls every few miles. (s32)

## The company and its companions

- Vorn, the shield guardian, waits outside Port Nyanzaru's walls whenever the company goes in, and only there: Wakanga O'tamu gave Urida the journal that led to him on the agreement that she would bring him back, and she means to keep him. (s7, s32)
- "Greg" or "Gregg" at the table is Grieg's echo, the Echo Knight's double. Grieg carries the driftglobe and has no darkvision. (s32)
- Mr. Stompy, Lilac Mist's racing dinosaur, was left at the Tiryki Anchorage pens to stud. (s32)
- Wickles, Chumble's familiar, is a pickle. He is invisible by default, can make himself seen and heard, and scouts at his own pace. (s32)
- Wickles and Vorn are not NPCs; see `site.not_npcs` in `build/authored/site.json`.

## NPCs

- Dragonbait is a saurial paladin who fights with a holy avenger longsword and a shield over a breastplate (ToA bestiary). The chronicle may call the sword holy.
- Dragonbait's scents are undeciphered by the party as of s32. Write the scent and, if Artus read it aloud, his reading. Never the meaning. (s32)
- Artus Cimber bears the Ring of Winter and seeks his lost wife (s4). He revealed himself aboard the ship Horizon, in its galley, dropping the room to a killing frost (s4); he and Dragonbait later held the Horizon's deck against pirates while his scroll carried the company clear (s25-26). Nothing of this happened in Waterdeep. He keeps an apartment at Tiryki Anchorage (s32). The Harpers' rank and file are decent; their leadership has him on a list of people to hunt down. (s32)
- Mesika, formerly "Nerissa": by her own account, ten days before she met the company she was on patrol outside a living Mezro, and she believed the year was 1385 DR. She had been in Port Nyanzaru for about a month under her own name, working with Wren the Harper on the Death Curse. She is not going to Omu. (s30, s31, s32) Her cast entry is keyed Mesika; Nerissa sits in `site.not_npcs` so the discovery pass never re-adds the borrowed name.
- The company never learned the spirit naga's name. (ruling, s30)
- The blue triangle mark: Chumble named it for Dendar the Night Serpent (s13). Artus says it was Mezro's mark for exiles and prisoners, taken by Ras Nsi for his own and used to brand his army of the dead. (s32)
- Xhosala Obanashi, mage hunter. Her household: Josok (priest), Ugor and Zamisi (scouts), Yapa and Dengo (acolytes). (s32)
- Rhoga's ghost told the Obanashi she felt Kolate still walked the realms; Xhosala suspects an undead remnant of him under the Forsaken Tree. This is her suspicion, not a fact the company has confirmed. (s32)
