Once in a Lifetime at Orolunga

Session XXVI 18 Marpenoth 20 SEP 2026 — no blade lifted —

Half a mile short of the ziggurat, Lilac Mist had found a small camp, its fire still smoking and no one at it, and then a voice at their backs. The camp was Artus Cimber's and Dragonbait's, the two the company had last seen on the Horizon's deck, turning to face the pirates as Artus's scroll carried everyone else clear. Understand what he is to them. He had told them before that night why he sailed: to ask Saja N'baza, the naga who guards Orolunga, after his lost wife. He and Dragonbait had spoken with her and camped here a week or so since. He could not go with them, because one sees her once in a lifetime and he had spent his. Asked about Nanny Pu'pu of Mbala, who calls the mistress of Orolunga an evil bitch woman and wants one of her scales as proof of a killing, he guessed at what Nanny herself might be: a hag, probably a green one.

Orolunga is a ziggurat of four tiers, and it is not climbed by climbing. The first stair turned dangerous and the company stopped, until Chumble Crudluck announced that a little guy, his words, had shown him the way, and the way was flowers. On the second tier another creature, again seen by the goblin, pointed him to a feather, and on the third a last one showed what both were for. The lesson had a snake in it. With great trepidation they tapped it with the flowers, stroked it with the feather, then lay down and let it slither into their mouths.

In Port Nyanzaru a stranger once fell against Chumble and whispered that the wise guardian of Orolunga could direct them to what they seek. She did. The Soulmonger, the thing that brought this company across the sea, is in Omu, a city hidden under the canopy where Acererak killed and buried nine trickster gods. The death curse that has made the dead so hard to raise ends when Ras Nsi dies: raised so often the curse has him too, Acererak's tool and intended victim, bent on opening a door under the Peaks of Flame to let Dendar the Night Serpent swallow the sun. That much was new. The rest she spent on what they already carried. She read them the legend of the nine gods, and Grieg matched it to the animals on the cube they carry for Vorn, their shield guardian, who may draw his power, she thought, from Omu itself. Shago, the guide hired at Fort Beluarian, carries an iron token, a death mark he called a mistake when a writ on his life surfaced in Port Nyanzaru, and could not say whose. She could. Kwayothe, one of the seven merchant princes, to punish Zhanthi, another. And the rod that turned up unexplained in the goblin's pack, ten runes lit by his kills and a new row Vex read as soul: a Rod of the Pact Keeper, she judged, corrupted by someone she could not name and would not have them trust. Then she reminded him that a warlock's gifts outlive his patron. Mark that she thought he needed to hear it.

Artus met them back at camp with the news that they had been gone all night. Now the company means to go back to Mbala for the hag. Nanny Pu'pu asked for a scale, and reached for a hair from the goblin's head while she asked. I do not think she will be getting either.

Chapter I

Passage to Chult

Azlund put them under a zone of truth in the House of Healing before he would say the words Death Curse, then paid them fifty gold and a map of Chult to go find its source. On the crossing they traced a missing wheel of cheese to a Zhentarim stowaway, which was enough to make the passenger calling himself Corwin admit he was Artus Cimber and wore the Ring of Winter. He teleported them off the deck when the pirates boarded, and they came down in the Harbor Ward of Port Nyanzaru without him. The weeks since have gone to rented dinosaurs and bought canoes while they collect the names of anyone who might know where the Soulmonger is.

Session I Sworn to Secrets, Bound for Chult — 1 Kythorn 15 MAR 2026

I have watched many a company assembled in the House of Healing, yet few so unlikely as this one. Vex, who assured all present that he was the only soul of good breeding in the room, came chiefly to outshine his cousin, while Grieg and Urida answered Azlund's postings in earnest and the goblin Chumble Crudluck answered chiefly the summons of a fruit bowl, from which a purloined banana found its way with impish precision into the seat of Vex's fine trousers just before he sat. They arrived in good time to catch Vander and Skamos holding a rapt crowd with tales of frost giants at Bryn Shander, of Artus Cimber, of the lost Harshnag and Hekaton, and the fall of Imyrith. Then Azlund, warmed near to tears by his cousin's arrival and much to Vex's private disgust, drew them upstairs and laid bare the great matter, the Death Curse that lets no dead soul rest, the Soulmonger at its root, and the long road south to Chult. For a modest purse apiece, a map of that green and hungry land, and the promise of far richer reward should they return victorious, the company took the charge, and so, a healing draught tucked away, they followed young Tessa down to the docks and found their ship, The Horizon, riding at anchor, where I do not doubt this tale of theirs truly begins.

Session II The Pickle, the Cheese, and the Storm — 5 Kythorn 22 MAR 2026

Ah, the crossing toward Chult aboard The Horizon, where I confess the finest theatre unfolded not in swordplay but inside a pickle barrel. The company came aboard under the watchful eye of Rindal, and the first evenings passed pleasantly enough, with young Chumble singing for his supper and earning himself a free beer while stout Grieg bested Corwin at sparring for a silver piece. Then the storm that Odele had feared came howling down, and the company answered it magnificently, Vex leaping to snatch Kessra from the waves as Anton braced him, Urida's unseen hand steadying both a loose crate and the wheel, and Grieg holding the ship to her course until the sea relented and a grateful captain draped a cloak of billowing across their shoulders.

Session IV A Stolen Cheese and a Hidden Ring — 28 Kythorn 12 APR 2026

Aboard a ship but two days out from Chult, my little goblin friend Chumble Crudluck set the whole affair spinning with a scheme of rare cunning, an arm wrestling contest contrived for no reason but to pry the ever-gloved Corwin out of his coverings. Session XXV The Last Survivor of Mbala ✝ 5 18 Marpenoth 13 SEP 2026

There are two ways to be the last soul left in a dead village. One is luck. Mark the other. Nanny Pu'pu, in the hut at Mbala's southern edge, said the pterafolk had killed everyone but her, and spared her only for being too stringy. She offers to raise the dead, and hers, she says, still get up, whatever trouble others are having. Understand what that boast was to a company sent across the sea because the dead have grown harder and harder to call back. That is the Death Curse. Her price for what she knew of Orolunga was the pterafolk dead. While she bargained, Wickles, Chumble Crudluck's familiar, found the clothes piled in her hut cut for several bodies, none hers.

Shago, the company's hired guide, went down the nest's chimney with Lilac Mist and Urida to ambush eight pterafolk while the rest waited above for whatever flew out. It nearly cost two of them. Lilac Mist fell and came back on an Ohelo berry, Urida on a potion Vex dropped on her. The pterafolk talked as they fought: they and the old woman had a standing quarrel over first bite of Mbala's people. The last survivor of Mbala was one of the two things that ate it.

When they rested, Chumble Crudluck found all ten runes lit on the mysterious rod he carries, a new row begun beneath. Vex read them. Soul.

Chumble Crudluck went back to report the pterafolk dead and ask after Orolunga. An evil place, she said, kept by an evil woman they could surely kill, and one of her scales would do as proof. Then her hand went for a hair on the goblin's head, and he caught it. Grieg and Lilac Mist, both covered in hair, both nearest her the first time, did their own arithmetic. The company agreed to look into Orolunga, to appease her, and withdrew.

Five days' travel later, half a mile short of Orolunga's ziggurat, Lilac Mist spotted a camp, its fire still smoking. Empty. As they searched, a voice somewhere said "Oh!", and Vex caught the smell of tar behind him. Dragonbait, who sailed with them from Waterdeep, speaks in scents, and tar is one of his words. And Dragonbait is seldom far from Artus Cimber, who spent a scroll to throw the company clear of a boarded ship and turned to meet the pirates with the Ring of Winter. They have not seen him since, and had every reason to expect him near. That much was expectation. The smell was not. They had not yet turned around, and already knew whose scent that was.

Session XXVI Once in a Lifetime at Orolunga — 18 Marpenoth 20 SEP 2026

Half a mile short of the ziggurat, Lilac Mist had found a small camp, its fire still smoking and no one at it, and then a voice at their backs. The camp was Artus Cimber's and Dragonbait's, the two the company had last seen on the Horizon's deck, turning to face the pirates as Artus's scroll carried everyone else clear. Understand what he is to them. He had told them before that night why he sailed: to ask Saja N'baza, the naga who guards Orolunga, after his lost wife. He and Dragonbait had spoken with her and camped here a week or so since. He could not go with them, because one sees her once in a lifetime and he had spent his. Asked about Nanny Pu'pu of Mbala, who calls the mistress of Orolunga an evil bitch woman and wants one of her scales as proof of a killing, he guessed at what Nanny herself might be: a hag, probably a green one.

Orolunga is a ziggurat of four tiers, and it is not climbed by climbing. The first stair turned dangerous and the company stopped, until Chumble Crudluck announced that a little guy, his words, had shown him the way, and the way was flowers. On the second tier another creature, again seen by the goblin, pointed him to a feather, and on the third a last one showed what both were for. The lesson had a snake in it. With great trepidation they tapped it with the flowers, stroked it with the feather, then lay down and let it slither into their mouths.

In Port Nyanzaru a stranger once fell against Chumble and whispered that the wise guardian of Orolunga could direct them to what they seek. She did. The Soulmonger, the thing that brought this company across the sea, is in Omu, a city hidden under the canopy where Acererak killed and buried nine trickster gods. The death curse that has made the dead so hard to raise ends when Ras Nsi dies: raised so often the curse has him too, Acererak's tool and intended victim, bent on opening a door under the Peaks of Flame to let Dendar the Night Serpent swallow the sun. That much was new. The rest she spent on what they already carried. She read them the legend of the nine gods, and Grieg matched it to the animals on the cube they carry for Vorn, their shield guardian, who may draw his power, she thought, from Omu itself. Shago, the guide hired at Fort Beluarian, carries an iron token, a death mark he called a mistake when a writ on his life surfaced in Port Nyanzaru, and could not say whose. She could. Kwayothe, one of the seven merchant princes, to punish Zhanthi, another. And the rod that turned up unexplained in the goblin's pack, ten runes lit by his kills and a new row Vex read as soul: a Rod of the Pact Keeper, she judged, corrupted by someone she could not name and would not have them trust. Then she reminded him that a warlock's gifts outlive his patron. Mark that she thought he needed to hear it.

Artus met them back at camp with the news that they had been gone all night. Now the company means to go back to Mbala for the hag. Nanny Pu'pu asked for a scale, and reached for a hair from the goblin's head while she asked. I do not think she will be getting either.
