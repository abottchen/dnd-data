Chapter I

Passage to Chult

From the House of Healing to the jungle's edge — by oath, by storm, by ice, by augury.

8 sessions 28 fallen
Session I An Oath, a Map, and a Sending Stone — 1 Kythorn 15 MAR 2026
No blade was drawn. The company took Azlund's oath under zone of truth, received the map of Chult and fifty gold apiece, and boarded The Horizon at the Waterdeep docks.
Session II The Horizon, and the First Storm — 5 Kythorn 22 MAR 2026
Days aboard the Horizon: Chumble hid in a pickle-barrel and woke with a familiar named Wickles. The storm came on the fourth day; six hands met it, and every rope held.
Session III Blades in the Salt ✝ 3 10 Kythorn 29 MAR 2026
Sahuagin boarded at dusk. Corwin and Dragonbait held the prow; the company took the stern. Four fell to the party — blade, mockery, and bell. A flash of blue light behind a crate was not commented upon.
Session IV The Ring, Revealed — 28 Kythorn 12 APR 2026
An arm-wrestle unmasked no secrets, but words did. Harlen was revealed a Zhentarim stowaway and the cheese-thief. Corwin showed his left hand, told his name, and named the Ring of Winter.
Session V Through Pirate Steel, into Nyanzaru ✝ 6 29 Kythorn 19 APR 2026
Pirates boarded in the night. The deck ran red. When it became clear the company could not win the tally, Artus Cimber teleported them to Port Nyanzaru in a flash of ice and turned alone to the remaining crew.
Session VI Three Riders and a Ring — 30 Kythorn 26 APR 2026
The party rented three dinosaurs from the Tiryki Anchorage pens, crossed paths with Volo Geddarm on the march to Goldenthrone where they heard the history of Chult and bought his book, and ran the city race circuit — Lilac Mist took first place, and Greig finished third to claim Ubtao's Ring.
Session VII Bones on the Steps of Tymora ✝ 19 1 Flamerule 03 MAY 2026
The party gathered intelligence from the innkeeper and Wakanga O'tamu—who gave Urida a journal and a task—then contracted Musharib as guide to Hrakhamar before cutting down a wave of undead attacking the Temple of Tymora in Malar's Throat.
Session VIII The Stranger Spoke Once, Then Slept — 4 Flamerule 10 MAY 2026
The party spent two days in Port Nyanzaru provisioning and gathering intelligence—visiting Grandfather Zitembe twice for visions and a briefing on the merchant princes, taking on Inete and Undril Silvertusk as companions bound for the jungle—before a stranger collapsed against Chumble in the market, whispered of the wise guardian of Orolunga, and woke the following morning with no memory of having spoken.
Chapter II

Down the Soshenstar to the Failing Camps

From the cooled town to the half-woken guardian — by canoe, by lasso, by spared goblin's oath, by creeper-fire, by the amulet off a dead queen's neck.

7 sessions 59 fallen
Session IX Down the Soshenstar by Canoe ✝ 2 11 Flamerule 17 MAY 2026
The party departed Port Nyanzaru on the fifth day, traveled the beach under Musharib's guidance, fended off two waves of flying monkeys, navigated a detour down a tributary where they recovered a magic ring from a Tyrannosaurus Zombie using Wickles as a lasso-bearer, and arrived one hex from Fort Righteous on the eleventh day with water supplies replenished.
Session X The Shrine Yields Its Jug ✝ 7 12 Flamerule 31 MAY 2026
The party braved the trapped shrine at Camp Righteous, weathering repeated explosions and falls to retrieve a brown ceramic jug, then turned back a goblin ambush led by Yokka, sparing the boss and her two surviving minions in exchange for intelligence on undead and a rival goblin tribe to the north.
Session XI Smoke Over Camp Vengeance ✝ 6 14 Flamerule 07 JUN 2026
The party arrived at a beleaguered Camp Vengeance, delivered Undril Silvertusk, investigated a strange enchanted plant in Wulf Rygor's tent, joined a sortie to destroy zombified soldiers bearing vine growths, and encountered a disoriented elven woman in the trees who spoke of fire and a dead shrine — then returned to find the camp under attack.
Session XII The Cave and the Creeper ✝ 6 15 Flamerule 14 JUN 2026
The party broke a yellow musk creeper's hold on Niles Breakbone, put down Wulf Rygor and his musk zombies, and traded armor and a canoe for a letter of service before learning that Musharib would require his daily rate to guide them off the sanctioned path.
Session XIII Throat Leeches and Honeyed Bones ✝ 14 21 Flamerule 21 JUN 2026
Traveling northeast along the River Soshenstar, the party survived a crocodile ambush that capsized their canoe and left most of them infested with throat leeches; after resting and receiving Undril's healing, they pressed north into the jungle, rescued a foolish thief staked out in honey for the ants, got lost by their guide, weathered a constrictor attack, and finally cut down a band of cannibals bearing a blue triangle mark linked by Chumble to Dendar the Night Serpent.
Session XIV The Goblins Had Flown Away ✝ 21 23 Flamerule 28 JUN 2026
The party shared camp with an Emerald Enclave patrol and found the goblins' abandoned clearing, where a wounded brontosaurus and a cut vine told them the camp had moved northeast. Their first attempt to distill dancing monkey fruit into arrow poison gassed the camp and yielded nothing. On the following morning they tracked and assaulted the Yellyark camp, killing Queen Grabstab and twenty goblins, but the survivors cut the vine and launched the camp southeast with the queen's body and amulet still aboard.
Session XV A Guardian Wakes, But Not Whole ✝ 3 27 Flamerule 05 JUL 2026
They found Yellyark reduced to wreckage, recovered Queen Grabstab's body and the command amulet, and buried the dead together. Two nights later eblis raided the camp before dawn and tried to carry off Inete; Wickles's warning brought the party to arms, three eblis fell, and the surviving two bought their lives with a marked path to a hidden ring. The next morning Lilac Mist spotted a wide bank of blue mist drifting toward camp, and the party skirted it. At Vorn the amulet worked only once Urida drew close, waking an adamantine guardian too depleted to do more than walk at her side, while a mud-caked box found by a nearby skeleton fit a slot on its frame without effect.
VThe Horizon

The Road Ahead

What is known, and where they turn.
What Is Known
  • The Ring of Winter — bound to Artus Cimber's hand, last seen opening an eye of frost on a deck of pirates.
  • The Soulmonger — engine of the Death Curse, somewhere in Chult.
  • The Death Curse — the dead will not stay raised, and the lately-raised waste in their flesh.
  • Azlund's Oath — fifty gold paid in hand, one rare gift each on return, if the Curse can be broken.
  • The Map of Chult — half-marked, given by Azlund at the Waterdeep docks.
  • The Sending Stone — Vex carries the pair, and Azlund waits at the other end.
  • Saja N'baza — a name in Chult, sought by Artus for word of his lost wife.
  • The Yuan-ti's Door — a yuan-ti drew blood and fled within; Wickles watches from the eaves.
Where They Turn

Yellyark found broken and empty, Grabstab's amulet in hand and Vorn the half-woken guardian shuffling at Urida's heel, the company turns back for Port Nyanzaru to resupply.
