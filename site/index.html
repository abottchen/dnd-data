Chapter II  ·  Down the Soshenstar to the Silent City

A Kindness Too Large by Half

Session XVIII 26 Eleasis 26 JUL 2026

Begin with the captain's apology, for it is the crooked thread running through this whole affair. The company overheard him quarrelling with his first mate over the tribute paid to Aremag. He offered back double what they had fed that dragon turtle, to be handed over when he collects them again. Then he put them ashore south of Shilku, clear of the volcanic debris raining down that coast, and left them a sending stone. Three days' notice, he said, and he would come. Generous. Suspiciously so. They walked north and found Shilku quiet above and stubbornly alive beneath, where Sithi Vinecutter opened a hatch and a history together. Her people want Hrakhamar back from the firenewts, and the treasury will answer for it. She asked after two scouts as well, Laz Drumthunder and Malkar Stonegrist, overdue out of the Valley of Lost Honor. On the road out, a salamander came at the company, and Chumble Crudluck unmade it with eldritch fire. Thirty miles short of Hrakhamar, Grieg finally set the pieces side by side. Days of sailing, a wait of any length, a voyage home, all freely given, and never one word about pirates. I have known cheaper kindnesses to cost a great deal more.

Session I Sworn to Secrets, Bound for Chult — 1 Kythorn 15 MAR 2026
I have watched many a company assembled in the House of Healing, yet few so unlikely as this one. Vex, who assured all present that he was the only soul of good breeding in the room, came chiefly to outshine his cousin, while Grieg and Urida answered Azlund's postings in earnest and the goblin Chumble Crudluck answered chiefly the summons of a fruit bowl, from which a purloined banana found its way with impish precision into the seat of Vex's fine trousers just before he sat. They arrived in good time to catch Vander and Skamos holding a rapt crowd with tales of frost giants at Bryn Shander, of Artus Cimber, of the lost Harshnag and Hekaton, and the fall of Imyrith. Then Azlund, warmed near to tears by his cousin's arrival and much to Vex's private disgust, drew them upstairs and laid bare the great matter, the Death Curse that lets no dead soul rest, the Soulmonger at its root, and the long road south to Chult. For a modest purse apiece, a map of that green and hungry land, and the promise of far richer reward should they return victorious, the company took the charge, and so, a healing draught tucked away, they followed young Tessa down to the docks and found their ship, The Horizon, riding at anchor, where I do not doubt this tale of theirs truly begins.

Session II The Pickle, the Cheese, and the Storm — 5 Kythorn 22 MAR 2026
Ah, the crossing toward Chult aboard The Horizon, where I confess the finest theatre unfolded not in swordplay but inside a pickle barrel. The company came aboard under the watchful eye of Rindal, and the first evenings passed pleasantly enough, with young Chumble singing for his supper and earning himself a free beer while stout Grieg bested Corwin at sparring for a silver piece. Yet a shadow crept into the galley when Pip the cook found a wheel of cheese gone missing and a bootprint far too large to be Pip's own, a small mystery I dearly hope to see resolved in some later chronicle of mine. Stranger still, Chumble spent a whole night hidden in the brine and woke to find his familiar reborn as a small pickle he christened Wickles, a companion I shall never tire of mentioning. Then the storm that Odele had feared came howling down, and the company answered it magnificently, Vex leaping to snatch Kessra from the waves as Anton braced him, Urida's unseen hand steadying both a loose crate and the wheel, and Grieg holding the ship to her course until the sea relented and a grateful captain draped a cloak of billowing across their shoulders.

Session III Suspicions Aboard, Sahuagin at the Rail ✝ 3 10 Kythorn 29 MAR 2026

Session IV A Stolen Cheese and a Hidden Ring — 28 Kythorn 12 APR 2026

Volo could tell you of a hundred nights at sea, but few close as this one did. Yet numbers are a cruel arithmetic, and when both ends of the ship were drowning in blades and even Grieg had been dropped by dagger work, it was Artus Cimber who made the choice that lingers in me still. He would not spend them all guarding a crew, so he pressed them toward their greater purpose, unfurled a scroll, and cast the company clear, the last sight afforded them being Artus wheeling to loose a mountain of ice from that famous ring upon the massed pirates. Session XI The Rot at Camp Vengeance ✝ 6 14 Flamerule 07 JUN 2026
Down the river the company pressed toward Camp Vengeance, hardened by the long road and each grown into deeper arts, pausing only at an abandoned campsite that gave up liquor, traveling gear, and clean water for the taking. Session XII The Fall of Camp Vengeance ✝ 6 15 Flamerule 14 JUN 2026
Wickles crept through the ash to find Camp Vengeance a ruin of smoke and corpses, with Ord Firebeard and Undril Silvertusk already trading blows against treacherous guards inside a tent, and our company swept round to the front to put the veteran and his three men down where they stood. From Ord they learned the whole calamity had been sown by Niles Breakbone and Wulf Rygor, who had fled north, and so northward the party pressed until they came upon poor wounded Perne Salhana, who gasped out word of a cave and the husks that lurked within it. Ah, but here is the beat I savor, for it was no simple treachery at all: a yellow musk creeper clinging to the cavern ceiling had Niles in its charm, and when Wickles opened with a flask of oil and the company's fire magic burned the vile plant to nothing, the poor man came back to his wits even as Wulf Rygor and his shambling musk zombies fought on to the bitter last. With the camp emptied to the river and its survivors bound for home, our heroes turned their eyes north once more toward evil goblins and a certain magic journal, though their guide Musharib, ever the practical soul, would want rather more coaxing and coin before he would stray from the safe path.

Session XIII The Soshenstar's Many Hungers ✝ 14 21 Flamerule 21 JUN 2026

Their guide Musharib, bless his hopeless sense of direction, promptly led them northwest into the coils of a constrictor snake before stumbling back onto the true path. Session XIV The Warren That Flew Away ✝ 21 23 Flamerule 28 JUN 2026
Even the jungle seemed to boil the day my companions pressed on toward the honeyman's promised site, where a priest of Mielikki named Nuanwa Olerro and three scouts of the Emerald Enclave shared shade and grim news that the walking dead, not the goblins, are the true rot of Chult, and that brave Camp Vengeance has fallen. They came at last upon a ruined clearing hung with skull-chimes and questioned a wounded brontosaurus, who gave up its own wisdom along with the spear pulled from its flank and swore the goblins had simply flown away, a riddle half-answered by a great cut vine and a tall tree bruised along its northeastern side. I must record, with a chronicler's fond exasperation, that their attempt to boil poison from dancing monkey fruit yielded nothing but a camp full of choking fumes and the humbling lesson that such alchemy wants an alembic. Come the cool of the next morning they crept upon the goblin warren of Yellyark beneath a silencing charm their guide Musharib had woven, and might have kept their surprise had Vex not tired of the waiting and loosed the first arrow himself, waking the whole nest to war. They cut down a score of goblins and put Lilac Mist's arrow through Queen Grabstab, yet the cunning wretches slashed a hidden vine and flung all Yellyark skyward toward the southeast, carrying the dead queen and the amulet at her throat clean out of reach, a theft I promise you shall echo in chronicles yet to come.
  • Vex breathed fire into a hut sheltering goblin children, and Urida's shatter killed those who survived.
  • At least six goblin children died there.
Session XV Ashes of Yellyark, a Guardian Half-Woken ✝ 3 27 Flamerule 05 JUL 2026
I confess a chronicler seldom relishes a graveyard, yet even I would have paused at what remained of Yellyark, beaten into utter wreckage with not one soul left breathing, where the company sifted the ruin, drew Queen Grabstab's command amulet from her cold remains, and gathered the fallen into some rough semblance of a funeral. The road that followed gave one quiet day and then a wicked night, for in the small hours five eblis swept down upon the camp and snatched Inete into the air, until Wickles' watchfulness roused Chumble Crudluck and the whole company besides, who felled the thief mid-flight and left their friend bruised from the drop but breathing. Three of the bird-fiends died before the surviving pair thought better of the business and bought their lives with a whispered map, which led to a ring tucked in a hollow tree, a trifling prize but honestly won. A creeping blue mist tried the party next, some forty feet of it drifting across their path, though Lilac Mist marked it in good time and the company slipped around rather than through. And at the last they found Vorn, that guardian of no small mystery, who stirred only when Urida drew near and even then answered but half its purpose, a great sentinel that walks and follows yet cannot strike nor ward, its power dwindled to embers beside a nameless skeleton and a mud-caked box of strange beast-faced carvings whose old magic had all but guttered out.
  • Queen Grabstab's command amulet was recovered from the wreckage of Yellyark.
  • Inete was snatched aloft by an eblis and fell about twenty feet but survived.
  • Two eblis were spared in exchange for the location of a hidden ring worth roughly fifty gold.
  • Vorn responds only when Urida is near and is only partially operational. It can walk and follow but cannot attack, store spells, or protect, and it heals only ten points a day.
  • A weak, nearly spent transmutation lingers on the mud-caked box, and it can be slotted into the guardian, though doing so produced no visible change.
  • The company plans to return to Port Nyanzaru to resupply.
Session XVI Long Road Home and the Dinosaur Races — 7 Eleasis 12 JUL 2026
The company's long march out of the deep jungle opened with a lesson in caution, for when Grieg caught the reek of carrion at a narrow canyon and poor Wickles was plucked from the sky by a hungry vine, the wise travellers simply walked the long way round. A gentler meeting followed in an almiraj, that shy one-horned hare, which Lilac soothed with honeyed animal-speech until it hopped trustingly into Vex's pack, and there I daresay it rides still. The river bore them back toward Port Nyanzaru, and though a giant boa constrictor chanced its luck against Vex mid-voyage, it slid away wounded into the dark water after Chumble's crackling blast, leaving the company to a well-earned spell of commerce, a scimitar sent off for enchanting and an alchemist's kit tucked away. Yet it is the dinosaur races that shall crown this chapter, where amid a farting triceratops, a plodding stegosaurus, and a banana dangled on a pole, our own Lilac Mist mounted Mr. Stompy to hushed whispers of her return and thundered to her third championship and a heavy purse of gold. And on that triumphant evening, as steadfast Musharib laid down his post to linger in town, a sly fellow named Rokah slipped from the shadows with a proposition wrapped in stolen charters and roguish promises, a hook I shall gladly leave dangling for my next telling.
  • No creatures were slain on this leg of the journey.
  • Wickles was snatched from the air by a vine, and the group detoured around the canyon rather than fight through the vines.
  • A giant boa constrictor seized Vex aboard the boat but could not pull him from it. Chumble wounded it with eldritch blast before it dove underwater and escaped.
  • The almiraj was calmed and befriended rather than killed and now rides in Vex's backpack.
  • The shield guardian was left a short way into the jungle, covered in roots and moss.
Session XVII The Long Road to Shilku Bay — 12 Eleasis 13 JUL 2026
Before the company quit the trading post they kitted themselves out in earnest, a keener scimitar, a stouter shield, arrows that bite truer, and the ever-blessed insect repellent, while young Grieg let go his old shield and the whole table traded a whispered rumor of a ruined city up the River Tath, guarded past Kahakla Gorge by a naga said to be both wise and generous. They signed on to escort a fellow named Rokah to Fort Beluarian, he footing their passage aboard the Pegasus, and once there they circled the walls like foxes about a henhouse and found no way to slip inside without inviting their own slaughter. Liara Portyr of the Flaming Fist put them to better use with bounties on ghoul heads, a mystery of monstrous footprints in the southern jungle, and the darkened silence of Shilku, and it was Shilku they chose to answer first. The sharpest ears among them caught wind of a man who reeked of ham and unraveled him to be Dragonbait himself, a dragon-man whose wrecked crew had begged passage home while one lonely prisoner went strangely unaccounted for. Then, with the guide Shago engaged and a Baldurian deck beneath them, they bought their way past the greedy Aremag with a scrape of coin and Vex's honeyed Draconic, Chumble tossing the tribute clean into the beast's maw, and by the time the coast fell away behind them the long road had hardened this company into something more dangerous than when first I met them.
  • Chumble Crudluck had a dream on the first night out.
  • Qawasha declined to guide the party for now, offering to join once they turn to fighting the undead.
  • Rokah took the failure at the fort badly and went back into the jungle with his companions rather than to his ship.
  • Wickles trailed Rokah's group afterward and marked that they were not heading back to their vessel.
Session XVIII A Kindness Too Large by Half ✝ 1 26 Eleasis 26 JUL 2026
Begin with the captain's apology, for it is the crooked thread running through this whole affair. The company overheard him quarrelling with his first mate over the tribute paid to Aremag. He offered back double what they had fed that dragon turtle, to be handed over when he collects them again. Then he put them ashore south of Shilku, clear of the volcanic debris raining down that coast, and left them a sending stone. Three days' notice, he said, and he would come. Generous. Suspiciously so. They walked north and found Shilku quiet above and stubbornly alive beneath, where Sithi Vinecutter opened a hatch and a history together. Her people want Hrakhamar back from the firenewts, and the treasury will answer for it. She asked after two scouts as well, Laz Drumthunder and Malkar Stonegrist, overdue out of the Valley of Lost Honor. On the road out, a salamander came at the company, and Chumble Crudluck unmade it with eldritch fire. Thirty miles short of Hrakhamar, Grieg finally set the pieces side by side. Days of sailing, a wait of any length, a voyage home, all freely given, and never one word about pirates. I have known cheaper kindnesses to cost a great deal more.

  • Barendd Torunn was dug out of a collapsed passage, given Mardred Ungart's adamantine pick, and paid the company in gems.

  • The company rested a night in the outpost's guest quarters before setting out.
  • Falling volcanic debris had to be dodged twice on the road toward Hrakhamar.
